# Juno Woods, PhD

<img alt="A photograph of Juno, who has Calico hair, grinning; with a white background" src="/assets/images/headshot.png" style="width:45%; float:right; margin: 0 0 1.5em 2em;" />

Aerospace engineer, AI researcher, and computational biologist. I architect spacecraft navigation systems and research how AI agents work in high-stakes environments. I bootstrapped the mission design for [Odysseus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IM-1) — the first privately-owned lunar lander on the Moon. I also make [interactive installations](https://www.resistanceaesthetic.com) and write about AI, biosecurity, and democracy.
